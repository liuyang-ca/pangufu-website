Euchre — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Euchre does not collect personal data through the app. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no game server. If you choose to email support, your message and the details you include are sent to us by email so we can respond.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your match in progress, your results and statistics, your hand history and replays, your coaching progress and any support note you are writing are saved on the device only. They are never sent to us. Settings > Reset progress deletes your settings, progress, statistics, history and match in progress; your purchase is kept. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Support notes

Nothing is sent automatically. A support note includes the app version and your rule settings, plus anything you type. It never includes a replay, your match in progress or your current hand. If you open the email composer, it is addressed to support@pangufu.ca and you choose whether to send it. You can also copy or share the note with any app you choose.

Replays

A completed hand's replay leaves the device only if you share it. It contains that hand's full deal and the decisions made in it, and goes only where you send it.

Purchases

The one-time Euchre Coach purchase is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Euchre never sees your payment details or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Euchre does not ask for a name, age or account, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: support@pangufu.ca.
