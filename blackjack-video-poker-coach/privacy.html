Blackjack & Video Poker Coach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Blackjack & Video Poker Coach does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

What stays on your device

Your settings (rules, look, sound), your practice stats and your list of missed hands are saved on the device only. They are never sent anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Blackjack & Video Poker Coach never sees your payment details or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Practice only

Blackjack & Video Poker Coach is a practice tool. It uses no real money and has no real-money play.

Children

Blackjack & Video Poker Coach collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
