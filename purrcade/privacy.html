Purrcade: Games for Cats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Purrcade does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound on or off, and the options chosen for each game) are saved on the device only. They are never sent anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Purrcade never sees your payment details or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Purrcade collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
