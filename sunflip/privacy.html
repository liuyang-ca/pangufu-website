Sunflip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Sunflip does not collect personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no server of ours. If you choose to email support, your message and any information you include are sent to us by email so we can respond. Share only what you want us to receive. We keep support emails only as long as we need them to answer and follow up, then delete them. To ask us to delete yours sooner, write to support@pangufu.ca.

Location

If you allow location, Sunflip sends your approximate location (to about a kilometre) to Apple to get the UV index for where you are, through Apple Weather (WeatherKit). If you type a place into the place search, the text is sent to Apple to find it. These requests go to Apple and nowhere else; Sunflip does not receive, keep or share them. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can refuse location and use the place search instead.

UV data from ARPANSA

When this source is turned on in a version of the app, Sunflip downloads ARPANSA's public UV feed, which lists every station. The download holds no location and no identifier; the app picks the nearest station on your device.

What stays on your device

Your sessions, routines, history, skin type, settings and the last place you chose are saved on the device only. They are never sent to us and are removed when you delete the app. They are not included in a support email unless you choose to include them.

Notifications

Turn-over, sunscreen and guideline alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Sunflip receives only whether the unlock is active. It never sees your payment details or your Apple Account.

Not medical advice

Sunflip is a timer. It does not measure your skin or the sun and does not give medical advice.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. A person who chooses to email support may include information in that message.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: support@pangufu.ca.
