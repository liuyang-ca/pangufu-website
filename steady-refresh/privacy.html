Steady Refresh — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Steady Refresh is a Chrome extension made by PANGUFU SOLUTION LTD. ("we"). It has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not run a server for it and we do not receive the pages you visit, your timers or your page rules.

What stays in your browser

Your timers, page rules (page addresses, keywords, number limits and elements to watch), settings, alert channels and the event log are saved in your browser's extension storage only. They are removed when you remove the extension. You can export the event log yourself from the Timers page.

Page content

Steady Refresh can read a page only on sites you allow, and only to do what you set up: check for a keyword, a number or a change in an element, or click an element after an alert. This check runs inside your browser. The page content is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What leaves your browser, and only when you turn it on

  • Sync across devices (Pro, off by default): your page rules are saved in Chrome sync, so they reach your other signed-in Chrome browsers. Google stores this data under your Google account, as covered by Google's privacy policy. Timers, alert channels, tokens and logs are never synced.
  • Off-device alerts (Pro): when an alert fires, the alert text (the kind of alert and, for a keyword alert, the keyword) is sent straight from your browser to the service you set up (your own webhook, Discord, Telegram or ntfy). The page address is included only if you add it to the message template. Your tokens and webhook addresses are used only to reach that service; they are saved in this browser alone, never synced or sent to us.

Purchases and trials

Pro trials and purchases are handled by ExtensionPay, and payments are processed by Stripe. When you start a trial you give them your email address, and when you pay, your payment details too; the extension never receives them. We can see your email address, your plan and your payment history in our ExtensionPay and Stripe accounts. We use them only to provide Pro, to answer support requests and to keep the records the law requires. We never see your full card number. To check your licence, the extension keeps an ExtensionPay key in Chrome sync storage and contacts extensionpay.com. It runs the ExtensionPay script only on extensionpay.com pages.

Chrome Web Store user data

Steady Refresh uses data only to provide the features you use. We do not sell or transfer user data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide creditworthiness or lending.

Children

Steady Refresh is not aimed at children. Nobody under 13 may buy Pro.

Your choices

You can turn sync and alert channels off, remove site access in Chrome, or remove the extension at any time. To ask what we hold about your purchase, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us. We may keep payment records as long as tax law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
