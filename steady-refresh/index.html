Browser extension · Chrome

Steady Refresh

Refresh a tab every few seconds, minutes or hours. Each tab has its own timer, and the icon badge shows the time left.

  • Randomize the wait, stop after a number of refreshes or at a set time
  • Skips a refresh while a form has unsaved text
  • Pauses when a site asks you to verify you are human

Free to use. Timers and keyword alerts stay free. Pro adds page monitors and phone alerts: 7 days free, then $14.99 once or $2.49 a month.
