1. Why did early European explorers call the native peoples they met "Indians"?
  1. They believed they had reached the East Indies
  2. It was an Inuit word for "neighbour"
  3. It came from the Huron-Wendat word for a visitor
  4. It honoured the explorers' patron saint
Show answer

Answer: A. They believed they had reached the East Indies

The first explorers thought they had landed in Asia, so they used a name tied to the East Indies.

2. Which people, described as nomadic, followed the bison herds?
  1. The Huron-Wendat
  2. The Sioux
  3. The Inuit
  4. The Iroquois

Answer: B. The Sioux

The Sioux moved with the bison, while the Huron-Wendat and Iroquois farmed and hunted.

  2. The Dene
  3. The Huron-Wendat
  4. The Sioux

The Cree and Dene of the Northwest lived by hunting and gathering instead.
